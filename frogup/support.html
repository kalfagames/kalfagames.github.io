Nasıl oynanır?

Telefonu sağa sola eğerek kurbağayı yürüt, ekrana dokunarak zıpla ve yukarıdaki kütüğe çık. Kütükler yavaşça aşağı iner; çamura gömülmeden önce yukarı tırman. Her yeni kütük puan kazandırır ve yükseldikçe bataklıktan uzaya yeni yerler açılır.

Kurbağa çok yavaş ya da çok hızlı dönüyor

Ayarlar > Hassasiyet bölümünden 1 ile 5 arasında seç. Daha büyük sayı, daha az eğerek daha hızlı dönmek demek.

How do I play?

Tilt your phone left and right to move the frog, tap the screen to jump up onto the next log. The logs slowly sink; keep climbing before the mud swallows you. Every new log scores a point, and the higher you go, the further you travel from the swamp into space.

The frog turns too slowly or too fast

Pick a level from 1 to 5 under Settings > Sensitivity. A higher number means faster turns with less tilt.

My best score is gone

Your best score and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them too.

Ads

The game is free and supported by ads. There are no ads while you play; the banner shows only on the main menu and the game over screen. Watching an ad to keep playing when a game ends is completely optional. In Europe you can change your ad consent under Settings > Privacy settings. On iPhone you can manage tracking permission in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
